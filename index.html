About PragatiOps

Progress, engineered — not just promised.

PragatiOps was founded on a simple idea: cloud infrastructure should be a source of confidence for a business, not a recurring source of anxiety. We work as an embedded extension of your engineering team, bringing hands-on DevOps and cloud expertise to organizations that need it without carrying the cost of a full in-house platform team.

Our name comes from pragati — progress. It's the standard we hold every engagement to: fewer manual steps, faster deployments, systems that recover on their own, and infrastructure that's documented and understood, not just running.

Built for production, from day oneWe design for the failure modes you'll actually hit, not the happy path in a demo.
Everything as codeInfrastructure, pipelines, and policies live in version control — reviewable and repeatable.
Security is not a phaseDevSecOps practices are part of the pipeline from the first commit, not bolted on later.
What we do

Cloud and DevOps services built around how your team ships.

From first migration to steady-state operations, we cover the full lifecycle of running infrastructure well.

Cloud migrationStrategy

Move workloads to the cloud — or between clouds — with a plan that accounts for downtime windows, data integrity, and cost, not just a lift-and-shift.

AWS, Azure & GCP setupFoundation

Well-architected account structures, networking, IAM, and security baselines set up correctly the first time, on whichever provider fits your workload.

CI/CD pipelinesDelivery

Automated build, test, and deployment pipelines that let your team ship confidently, multiple times a day if needed, with rollback built in.

KubernetesOrchestration

Cluster design, deployment, and ongoing management — from a single production cluster to multi-region, multi-tenant setups.

Infrastructure as CodeAutomation

Terraform and equivalent tooling to define every environment in code, so provisioning is repeatable, reviewable, and disaster recovery is a deploy, not a scramble.

Cost optimizationEfficiency

Right-sizing, reserved capacity planning, and waste elimination — most engagements pay for themselves in savings within the first few months.

24/7 monitoringReliability

Observability stacks and alerting tuned to catch real problems early, with on-call support so incidents get resolved before customers notice.

DevSecOpsSecurity

Security scanning, secrets management, and compliance controls integrated directly into your pipelines rather than handled as a separate audit.
